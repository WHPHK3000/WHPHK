HongKong Whampoa
Industrial Technology Co., Ltd.

Full-Chain Services, Value Co-Creation

The business operations of Hong Kong Whampoa Technology are extensive and in-depth, aimed at creating comprehensive value for clients:

Core Business

01

High-End Construction Consulting and Technology Empowerment

Planning and Design: Leveraging the expertise of China’s top-tier Grade A design institutes combined with renowned international design firms, we provide world-class services in urban planning, architectural design, landscape design, interior design, and specialized engineering design, integrating innovative concepts with localized practices.
Project Management and Engineering Consulting: Offering professional management consulting services throughout the entire project lifecycle, including project planning, feasibility studies, cost control (quantity surveying), bidding consultation, and project management.
Professional Advisory Consulting: Providing in-depth services such as policy and regulatory interpretation (particularly in cross-border investment and construction), market entry strategies, technical standards alignment, and sustainability consulting (green building, ESG).

02

Global Building Materials Trade and Supply Chain Integration

Actively introducing green, eco-friendly, and smart new building materials and technologies to build an efficient and reliable international building materials trade network, covering B2B and B2C new material supply.
Fully leveraging Hong Kong’s advantages as a free port and trade hub, our services include: material selection recommendations, global sourcing of new building materials, quality control, logistics and customs clearance, and supply chain financial solutions.

03

Cutting-Edge Technology R&D and Application Investment

Blockchain Technology and Related Innovative Fields:

  • Real-World Assets (RWA)
  • Digital Asset Management
  • Decentralized Applications (DApps)
  • Smart Contracts and Other Cutting-Edge Technology Enterprises and Projects

Deep Application of Artificial Intelligence (AI):

  • Smart Design and Optimization: Building performance simulation, design solution optimization
  • Smart Construction Site Management: Safety monitoring, progress tracking, quality control
  • Digital Twin: Real-world digital twin simulation
  • Robotics: Intelligent robotics industry

Life Sciences & Healthcare:

  • Stem Cell & Regenerative Engineering
  • Anti-Aging
  • Wellness Industry
  • TCM & Therapy
